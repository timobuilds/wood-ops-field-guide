# Cover critical work across shifts

## Job to be done

**Situation.** Absence, turnover, seasonal peaks or dependence on one expert threatens delivery.

**Deliverable.** A skills matrix and a targeted cross-training plan with demonstration criteria.

Name who needs progress, in what circumstance, and how they will measure it. Use the known baseline or identify the smallest observation that would establish one. Label proposed targets.

## Work through the decision

1. List critical tasks, people, shifts and required coverage. Use observable proficiency levels such as beginner, independent operator, advanced operator and instructor. Separate verified competence from self-report.

2. Map single-person dependencies and upcoming gaps against demand. Pick one learner, qualified trainer and protected training slot. Capture the expert’s setup checks and common exceptions.

3. Require observed performance under the plant’s training rules. Review coverage after training and test the roster for an absence. Keep individual assessments available only to the intended audience.

## Return to the user

Fill in the deliverable with the evidence at hand. Mark estimates and unknowns. Name the next action, its proposed owner, the review point and the finding that could change the decision. Cite the relevant column for its teaching and identify your proposed application.

**Example request.** Only one person can set up the edgebander on second shift.
