# Recover usable material

## Job to be done

**Situation.** Lumber waste or high material consumption threatens the cost per acceptable component.

**Deliverable.** A material balance and a yield trial for one product family.

Name who needs progress, in what circumstance, and how they will measure it. Use the known baseline or identify the smallest observation that would establish one. Label proposed targets.

## Work through the decision

1. Define a boundary and consistent units for purchased input and good output. Record grade, dimensions, order mix, opening/closing inventory and reusable offcuts so purchase timing does not distort yield.

2. Follow losses across cutting, machining, grading, gluing and rejection. Distinguish unavoidable allowance from defects found after additional work. Evaluate delivered cost per usable part across grades.

3. Trial one change in grade, cutting pattern, kerf or earlier inspection. Compare good output and cost per good part at comparable mix, with a quality check.

## Return to the user

Fill in the deliverable with the evidence at hand. Mark estimates and unknowns. Name the next action, its proposed owner, the review point and the finding that could change the decision. Cite the relevant column for its teaching and identify your proposed application.

**Example request.** Cheaper lumber increased our scrap. Which grade actually costs less per part?
