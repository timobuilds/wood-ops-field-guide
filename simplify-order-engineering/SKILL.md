# Turn custom orders into reliable work instructions

## Job to be done

**Situation.** Customer options, disconnected software or manual re-entry slow order engineering.

**Deliverable.** A mapped order-information flow and a pilot for one reusable rule or integration.

Name who needs progress, in what circumstance, and how they will measure it. Use the known baseline or identify the smallest observation that would establish one. Label proposed targets.

## Work through the decision

1. Trace one variant from customer selection through design, costing, bill of materials, labels and machine instructions. Count re-entry, decisions, corrections and elapsed time. Include front-office effort per comparable order.

2. Separate standard product rules from genuine exceptions. Identify the authoritative data and the interfaces between configurator, CAD, ERP and production. Check identifiers, revisions, units and actual interface support.

3. Pilot one family from choice to validated instructions. Verify physical fit, exception handling, change propagation and downstream completion. Treat the Industry 4.0 columns as historical context and verify current product capabilities on the project.

## Return to the user

Fill in the deliverable with the evidence at hand. Mark estimates and unknowns. Name the next action, its proposed owner, the review point and the finding that could change the decision. Cite the relevant column for its teaching and identify your proposed application.

**Example request.** Every custom cabinet gets keyed into three systems. Where should we start?
